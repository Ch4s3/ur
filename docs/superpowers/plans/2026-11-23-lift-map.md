# Lift Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Storms in Ur grow where land/sea temperature, breezes, slopes and city heat lift the air, instead of entering from the map edge at random.

**Architecture:** A new pure module `Ur.Air` turns heights, date, regional weather and a per-parcel city-heat array into an `Air` record of int arrays (coast field, shadow flags, five lift snapshots). `Ur.Rain.cells` takes the lift snapshots and births cells from them. `Ur.Sim` computes air + storms once per day ("dawn"), caches them on the city with the day they were made for, and saves them.

**Tech Stack:** March, forge (`forge test --release <file>`), Strata.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-lift-map-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Map 60×44 parcels of 8 m (480×352 m); sea is `heights == 0`; lift grid 15×11 areas of 4×4 parcels; 5 snapshots at hours 2, 6, 10, 14, 18.
- Rain levels 0 / 2 / 3 / 4; `Ur.Rain.Cell` keeps its fields.
- `Ur.Air.Air` holds only Int fields and NativeIntArr (Float record fields read across modules can be misread — see the note above `wind_speed_on_day`).
- `temperature(c)`, `local_temperature`, `wind_at`, `Ur.Sea`, uniform-rain cities and Idra's curse are unchanged.
- Island-wide rain units per kind 2–4, averaged over a year, within ±20 % of the pre-change figure.
- City heat changes storm odds near the core by less than 20 %.
- Older saves may break.

## Review Focus

1. A brand-new city (constructor, scenario, setup screen) has no dawn yet — rain and clouds must still work on its first frame (`ensure_dawn`).
2. Maps with no land or no sea (tests build tiny/flat cities) — coast field and lift must not divide by zero or index out of range.
3. A save made mid-day after grading reloads with identical `rain_now` for the rest of that day.
4. Severe days still flood the way the flood tests expect (front + cells).
5. Frame cost: `day_cells` per frame must read the cache, never rebuild the lift map.

**Interfaces — Produces:**

- `empty() : Air`
- `sea_temp(month : Int, climate_heat : Int) : Int`

- `shadow(heights : NativeIntArr, heading : Float) : NativeIntArr`
- `breeze(air : Air, lot : Int, hour : Int) : (Int, Int)` (km/h, x and z)
- `build(day : Int, heights : NativeIntArr, heat : NativeIntArr, month : Int, kind : Int, daily : Int, soil : Int, climate_heat : Int, wind_speed : Int, heading : Float) : Air`
- `lift_at(air : Air, snapshot : Int, area : Int) : Int`

- [ ] Write `air_checks`: sea > land at month 0 hour 5; land > sea at month 6 hour 15; on a test island (round hill in the middle), a coastal parcel's breeze points inland at 15:00 in month 6 and outward at 3:00; the windward slope's area has higher lift at snapshot 14:00 than the lee's; a lee parcel has shadow 1 and a windward parcel 0; an all-sea and an all-land map build without crashing.
- [ ] Run `forge test --release test/ur_test.march` → FAIL (module missing).
- [ ] Implement. Sea: `250 + 2/3 × (season(month−1) − 270) + climate_heat×10`, season as in `temperature_on`. Land: `daily×10 + swing × sin-curve(low 05, high 15)`, swing 60/30/15 by clear/cloudy/rain, ×(130−soil)/100. Coast: multi-pass distance-to-sea capped at 12, inland vector = negated gradient normalised. Shadow: march ≤10 parcels upwind; shadow when max upwind height − own height ≥ 24 (quarter-metres → 6 m). Lift per area: heating (land: max(0, land−sea); sea within 3 areas of land: max(0, sea−land)) × moisture factor on land + convergence (sum over 4 neighbours of breeze pointing in) + orographic (wind · height gradient, signed) + mean city heat.
- [ ] Run → PASS. Commit "Air: land and sea temperature, breezes, lift and rain shadow".
